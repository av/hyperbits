# flying-through-words

Category: scenes-3d. Fidelity 1 -> 5 (1-5, judged against the remotion-bits original rendered side by side).

Was: eight words in fixed lanes flown past by a stepped camera.
Now: words are particles (rate 0.2, lifespan 100, z velocity 10vmin +/- 10vmin from a -50vmin deep spawn box) in a 5000px perspective container with the [0, 1, 0.5, 0.2, 0] opacity curve. Every word spawns where the original's does; diff 0.0.
